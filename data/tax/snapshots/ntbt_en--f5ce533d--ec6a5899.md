Documents Required

WHAT KIND OF DOCUMENTS SHOULD BE SUBMITTED WHEN FILING THE INDIVIDUAL HOUSE AND LAND TRANSACTIONS INCOME TAX RETURN?

- The Individual House and Land Transactions Income Tax return form with the taxpayer's signature, or the agent's signature, if an agent is appointed by the taxpayer. The tax payable, if any, the payment receipt shall be attached as well.

- The photocopies of both the properties or house utilization right procurement and sales contracts.

- Documents which can provide evidence of the transaction costs and expenses necessary for acquisition of the house and land, and other relevant supplementary documents.
