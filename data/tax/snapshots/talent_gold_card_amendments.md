## English：Taiwan Gold Card: New Amendments, Stronger Advantages

Launched in 2018,

The Employment Gold Card

is a powerful, combined residence visa, work permit, Alien Resident Certificate (ARC), and re-entry permit into one—typically valid for one to three years, with extensions available. Designed to make professional life, daily living, and international travel smoother, the Gold Card reflects Taiwan’s growing commitment to attracting top global talent.

Following the Legislative Yuan's adoption of the latest amendments to the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals, Gold Card holders—who already enjoy numerous conveniences—can look forward to even more attractive benefits. These legislative updates are designed

to accelerate the path to permanent residency, provide more flexible family residency options, and extend personal income tax incentives, offering a stronger legal foundation and a smoother transition for long-term career and family development in Taiwan.

New Amendments: Major Advantages for Gold Card Holders

The significant updates in this legislation aim to provide Gold Card holders with greater certainty, flexibility, and advantages regarding their career development and family life in Taiwan. These changes directly address the critical pain points top talent often considers when planning long-term relocation.

1. Accelerated Permanent Residency: A Shorter Path to Long-Term Stability

One of the most impactful changes is the

substantial reduction in the number of years of required residency for Gold Card holders to apply for the Alien Permanent Resident Certificate (APRC).

This adjustment acknowledges the contributions of highly skilled professionals, expediting the long-term goal of permanent residency.

2. Enhanced Family Flexibility and Security

The revised legislation eases restrictions on the residency of Gold Card holders' family members (dependents, such as spouses and children), substantially strengthening family stability.

Spouse Residency and Work Rights:

Spouses of Gold Card holders will now have residency permits that offer greater flexibility and can

directly apply for an open work permit

, free from employer restrictions. This greatly enhances the possibility of pursuing dual careers in Taiwan.

Children's Residency:

Related provisions for dependent children are relaxed, ensuring their residency rights are protected and not jeopardized by potential changes in the primary Gold Card holder's status, guaranteeing continuity in their education in Taiwan.

These comprehensive legislative updates successfully elevate the Gold Card from a simple work visa to a

holistic family development and permanent residency pathway

, powerfully affirming Taiwan's commitment to welcoming the world's top professionals.

Gold Card: More Than a Permit, It's the 'Made It in Taiwan' Pathway

The Taiwan Employment Gold Card has become more than a work permit—it’s a pathway for global professionals to build lasting careers and lives in Taiwan. For experts in fields from research to entrepreneurship, it offers freedom, stability, and a vibrant international community.

With the recent amendments to the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals, Gold Card holders now enjoy an accelerated path to permanent residency, extended tax incentives, family benefits, and greater employment flexibility.

Personalized Support: Talent Taiwan's Consultation Services

As part of its commitment to supporting Gold Card holders throughout their journey in Taiwan, Talent Taiwan bilingual help desk provides a wide range of personalized consultation services. Professionals can

book one-on-one sessions

—both online and in person—in Taipei or Kaohsiung to receive guidance on visas, work permits, Gold Card and APRC applications, company establishment, personal taxation, and business-related issues such as hiring and contract matters. Dedicated tax consultations and business advisory sessions are also offered regularly, ensuring that Gold Card holders have access to reliable, up-to-date information at every stage of their life and career planning in Taiwan.

For inquiries or to schedule a consultation, please email us at

the Taiwan Employment Gold Card Office

With the recent amendments to the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals underway, our mission remains the same—to make Taiwan not only a place to work, but a place to grow and belong. Beyond visas, we provide ongoing support with employment, housing, education, and taxation, helping every professional confidently build a long-term future in Taiwan.

## 中文：台灣就業金卡：新修法完整升級您的在台生活與工作

就業金卡

於 2018 年推出，是一張整合了居留簽證、工作許可、外僑居留證（ARC）與重入國許可於一身的多功能證件，效期通常為一至三年，且可延展。這張卡的設計目的，是讓外籍專業人士在台灣的工作、生活與國際往返更加便利，也展現台灣積極吸引全球頂尖人才的決心。

隨著立法院近期三讀通過《外國專業人才延攬及僱用法》最新修法，原先就享有多項便利措施的金卡持有者，將迎來更具吸引力的待遇。新的修法項目將加速國際人才取得永久居留的途徑、提供更彈性的家庭居留選項、並延長個人所得稅優惠，為外籍人才在台灣的職涯發展與家庭規劃提供更完善的法律基礎與順暢銜接。

新修法重點：重要優勢助攻金卡申請人

本次修法的核心，是讓金卡持有者在台灣的職涯與家庭規劃更具穩定性、彈性與誘因，並回應高階人才在評估長期移居時最在意的關鍵因素。

1. 永居更快取得：加速邁向長期落腳

本次最關鍵的更新項目之一，是

大幅縮短金卡持有人申請永久居留（APRC）的居留年限要求

。這項調整反映台灣對高階專業貢獻的肯定，也讓有意長期發展的人才能更快達成落地生根的目標。

2.

家庭彈性與保障全面提升

新版法規放寬了金卡持有人家庭成員（如配偶與子女）的居留限制，強化整體的家庭穩定度。

配偶的居留與工作權

：配偶的居留證將更具彈性，可直接申請開放式工作證，不再受雇主限制，能順利讓申請人及配偶在台追求各自的職涯發展。

子女的居留保障

：子女的相關居留規定也同步放寛，其居留權不會因金卡持有人身分變動而受影響，確保在台能順利就學不中斷。

這次的制度全面升級，使就業金卡不再只是工作簽證，更是涵蓋家庭發展與永久居留的完整規劃，充分展現台灣吸引國際優秀人才的決心。

金卡：不只是許可證，而是一條「Made It in Taiwan」的實踐路徑

台灣就業金卡不僅是工作許可，而是讓全球專業人士在此扎根、發展、生活的完整途徑。無論是學術研究、創業或跨領域專業，金卡都提供更高的自由度、穩定性，以及充滿活力的國際社群。

隨著《外國專業人才延攬及僱用法》修法，金卡持有人擁有更快的永久居留途徑、更多的稅務優惠及家庭友善措施與工作彈性。

一對一諮詢服務
