## English：Taiwan’s New Rules for Global Talent: Six Key Highlights

On August 29, 2025, Taiwan’s Legislative Yuan passed amendments to the

Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals

. The changes aim to make it easier for international professionals and students to build careers and long-term futures in Taiwan, while also strengthening social protections.

Here are the six key highlights:

Easier Pathways for Overseas Compatriot and International Students

Overseas compatriots and international students who earn an associate degree or higher in Taiwan may apply to extend their residency for one year after graduation. If needed, they may apply for a second extension, for a maximum total of two years—without needing to apply for a work permit.

Opening Doors for Young Global Talent

To attract graduates from the world's top 200 universities, this amendment allows them to apply for an individual work permit, giving them the freedom to legally engage in professional work in Taiwan.

- Normally, foreigners working in “specialized or technical fields” need 2 years of experience. The work experience exemption now extends from graduates of the top 500 universities to graduates of the top 1,500 universities, especially helping young professionals from New Southbound countries.

Digital Nomads Can Stay Longer

The digital nomad visa now allows stays of up to

2 years

, up from the previous 6 months.

Faster Access to Permanent Residency

When applying for permanent residency, overseas compatriots who hold an associate's degree or higher can shorten their required continuous residency period by 1 to 3 years.

- High-earning professionals (annual income above NT$6 million) can apply for permanent residency after just

1 year of legal residency

.

Stronger Social and Job Security

Foreign professionals can now join the

new labor pension system

, even without permanent residence.

- Those with permanent residence will also be covered by employment insurance, including unemployment benefits and parental leave subsidies.

Long-term Care and Disability Services

Foreign professionals with permanent residence who have lived in Taiwan for over 10 years can access disability and long-term care services, offering support equal to that of citizens.

Glossary

Foreign Professionals

Foreign nationals who are qualified to work in a specialized field in Taiwan based on their education and work experience.

Foreign Special Professionals

A more advanced group of foreign professionals with special expertise in a field that is in high demand in Taiwan. They are a highly sought-after group the government aims to attract.

For the full legal definitions of “Foreign Professionals” and “Foreign Special Professionals,” please refer to

this official source

.

In short, these reforms are not only about attracting more international professionals to Taiwan, but also about helping them build stable careers, enjoy stronger protections, and feel supported in making Taiwan their “second home.”

To make this possible, the NDC is working with other ministries to finalize the related regulations, with the goal of implementing the law and all supporting rules together in early 2026.

## 中文：台灣放寬《外專法》規定：六大修法亮點

2025 年 8 月 29 日，立法院三讀通過

《外國專業人才延攬及僱用法》

修正草案。這次修法的重點，在於讓更多國際專業人才與外籍學生能更容易在台灣發展職涯、安心久居，同時也加強社會保障制度。

以下是六大修法重點：

1. 僑外生畢業後留台更容易

在台灣取得副學士以上學位的僑外生與國際學生，畢業後可以向移民署申請延長居留 1 年；若仍有需要，可再申請一次，最多可延長共 2 年。在台工作不再需要申請工作許可。

2. 向全球青年人才敞開大門

世界前 200 大學的畢業生，可直接申請個人工作許可，自由從事專業工作。

此外，原本需有兩年工作經驗的規定，現在放寬至世界前 1,500 大學畢業生可免除，有助於新南向國家的青年專業人才來台發展。

3. 數位遊牧簽證大幅延長

數位遊牧簽證的最長停留時間，從原本的 6 個月延長至 2 年。

4. 永久居留申請更快更簡單

在台取得副學士以上學位的僑外生，申請永久居留時可折抵 1–3 年的連續居留年限。

同時，高收入專業人士（年薪 600 萬元以上）可在台居留滿 1 年後就申請永久居留，加速高階人才留台。

5. 就業與社會保障全面升級

外國專業人才即使尚未取得永久居留，也能納入新制勞退制度，確保退休金年資不中斷。

取得永久居留者，還能參加就業保險，包括失業給付與育嬰留職停薪津貼等保障。

6. 提供長照與身心障礙服務

取得永久居留並在台居留滿 10 年的外籍專業人才，可使用長照與身心障礙照護服務，享有與本國人同等的支持，減輕家庭負擔。

📘重要名詞解釋

外國專業人才

指依其學歷與工作經驗，符合資格在台灣從事特定專業領域工作的外籍人士。

外國特定專業人才

相較一般外國專業人才更具專業性，擁有台灣高度需求領域的特殊專長，為政府積極延攬的對象。
