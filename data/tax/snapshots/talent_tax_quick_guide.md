## English：Filing Taxes in Taiwan: A Quick Guide for Expats

In Taiwan, tax season runs from

May 1 to May 31

each year. For expats living and working here, filing taxes for the first time can feel confusing or overwhelming. Whether you're employed by a local company or earning income from abroad, understanding your tax responsibilities is key. This guide breaks down the essential questions to ease the process:

Do foreigners need to file taxes in Taiwan?

Yes. If you earn income in Taiwan, you're required to pay taxes—regardless of your nationality. Your tax obligations depend on the number of days you’ve stayed in Taiwan during the calendar year.

How is tax calculated based on stay duration?

Resident (183 days or more in a calendar year):

You must file an annual income tax return in May of the following year. This includes salary from overseas employers if the work was done in Taiwan.

Non-resident (less than 183 days):

(1)

90 days or less:

Taxes are withheld at the source by your employer. No need to file a return.

(2)

91–182 days:

Some income, especially not subject to withholding, must still be declared and taxed.

Can Taiwan taxes be used for foreign tax credit?

It depends on your home country’s tax laws. You may apply for a tax payment certificate from Taiwan’s National Taxation Bureau to check whether your home country allows tax credits for income taxed in Taiwan.

When should you file taxes?

From

May 1 to May 31

each year. If you’re leaving Taiwan permanently before then, you must file before your departure. If you appoint someone to file on your behalf, you’ll need to prepare an authorization letter and relevant documents.

Where to file?

Expats should file at the local tax office corresponding to the address on their ARC (Alien Resident Certificate). If you’re married to a Taiwanese citizen and wish to file jointly, you may choose either your spouse’s household registration district or your own residence.

Where to File Online: Use Taiwan’s E-Filing System

For a faster and more convenient way to file your taxes, expats in Taiwan can use

the Ministry of Finance’s official e-Filing system

. The platform, known as E-Fill, allows you to download tax filing software, check instructions, and access the latest updates on income tax filing. While the interface is primarily in Chinese, you can still use it with help from local friends or consult your local tax office for guidance.

Do you need to file taxes if you live abroad?

Yes, if you have income from Taiwanese sources (e.g., business profits, interest, or investments), taxes may still apply—even if you're not physically in Taiwan. Tax may be withheld by the payer, but if not, you are responsible for declaring and paying it.

For more assistance, consult your local National Taxation Bureau office or visit the

Ministry of Finance’s tax portal

. The Talent Taiwan website also offers a dedicated

FAQ section on taxes

. With its headquarters in Taipei and a branch office in Kaohsiung, Talent Taiwan welcomes inquiries from all regions. If you have any questions, feel free to contact the

Talent Taiwan helpdesk

—our team is here to support you.

## 中文：台灣報稅簡易指南

在台灣，一年一度的報稅季為每年

5 月 1 日至 5 月 31 日

。對於居住並在台工作的外籍人士來說，首次報稅可能會感到困惑或壓力大。然而，無論您是受僱於台灣公司，或是從海外賺取收入，了解自己的納稅義務都是相當重要的。本文統整了以下關鍵問題，協助您更順利地完成報稅流程：

一、外國人也要報稅嗎？

是的

。只要外籍人士在台有賺取收入，就需要依照規定納稅。課稅方式會根據當年度「在台居留日數」而有所不同。

二、課稅方式一覽

依據居留日數分為「居住者」與「非居住者」：

居住者

（當年度在台超過 183 天）：

➤ 需在隔年 5 月辦理

綜合所得稅結算申報

，包括境外雇主給付的勞務報酬。

非居住者

：

➤ 居留

不超過 90 天

：以「就源扣繳」方式處理，由雇主代扣代繳，不需報稅。

➤ 居留

91 - 182 天

：部分收入仍須申報，尤其是非扣繳範圍內的收入。

三、在台繳稅可在國外抵稅嗎？

這取決於您母國的稅法。可向台灣國稅局申請「納稅證明書」，並向當地稅務機關確認是否可用於海外稅額抵扣。

四、什麼時候報稅？

- 每年 5 月 1 日至 5 月 31 日（如中途離境，需於離境前申報）。

- 若委託他人代為申報，需準備委託書與相關證明文件。

五、去哪裡報稅？

- 外籍人士需依照「居留證上的地址」至對應的國稅局申報。

- 若與台籍配偶合併申報，可選擇配偶戶籍地或自身居留地的國稅局辦理。

六、線上報稅好幫手：E-Fill 報稅平台

想要更快速、方便地完成報稅，外籍人士可以使用財政部推出的

線上報稅系統

。這個平台提供報稅軟體下載、使用說明與最新申報資訊。雖然操作介面以中文為主，但可請教熟悉中文的朋友協助，或向當地國稅局諮詢說明，讓報稅流程更加順利。

七、不在台灣也要報稅嗎？

需要。如果您有台灣來源的收入（例如：投資利潤、利息、股息等），即使人不在台灣，也可能需繳納所得稅。若未由支付單位代扣稅款，您有責任自行申報與繳稅。
