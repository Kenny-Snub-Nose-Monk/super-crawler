Days in Taiwan, n.

How many days have you spent in Taiwan this tax year?

212days, 1 January to 23 October

A foreigner without a household in Taiwan who stays 183 days or more in one tax year is a resident, and is taxed on Taiwan income at progressive rates. The day of arrival is not counted; the day of departure is.
